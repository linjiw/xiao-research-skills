# Learn research and writing decisions from papers

Use for source-based teaching, comparative reading, or literature synthesis. Use `source-retrieval.md` for corpus access and version checks; use the deliberate-practice loop in `argument-workshop.md` when the user wants an exercise. This guide offers analytical methods, not new claims of corpus reading.

## Read for a decision

Choose the uncertainty the reading should resolve: why a question matters, what an experiment identifies, how a paper connects evidence to a claim, or what a paragraph helps the reader understand. Read the passages necessary for that decision. An abstract can help triage relevance; it cannot establish every implementation choice or experimental limitation.

Keep source observation separate from interpretation. Useful working notes are:

| Source and inspected location | What it actually shows | Your inferred lesson | When the lesson fails | Decision for this project |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Version, pages, and access scope | Claim, comparison, or writing move | Reason the move may help | Missing assumption or counterexample | Proposed change and how to check it |

Use only fields that help the task. Do not turn a small teaching request into a full literature database. If only a ledger entry or abstract was available, label that scope and leave unverified details unresolved.

## Study the reasoning behind a successful move

For research craft, ask which alternative the authors had to distinguish and what observation could have changed their conclusion. Look for the comparator, measurement, or assumption doing the inferential work. A persuasive motivation is not evidence that a mechanism works.

For writing craft, identify what the reader knows before a passage and what they can understand afterward. Explain how the passage connects a concrete problem to a design, introduces a definition before using it, or places a qualification beside a result. Transfer that function into original prose about the user's facts. Do not copy distinctive phrasing or treat acronym style, paragraph order, or venue acceptance as evidence that a writing choice caused success.

When a proposed rule seems universal, seek a contrasting case. The existing `source-patterns.md` pairs a component-isolating comparison with an integrated-system study that leaves component effects open. That contrast supports a conditional lesson: choose attribution controls when the claim requires attribution. Verify the original passages before teaching their specifics.

## Synthesize around the question

For novelty or related work, search primary literature beyond this author corpus using the problem, competing method names, and relevant assumptions. Follow citations when they identify a closer comparator. Report the search date, scope, and material access limits when making a novelty assessment; a search miss does not prove absence.

Compare papers on dimensions that affect the user's claim: task and population, training and runtime information, comparator resources, outcome definitions, evidence type, and remaining uncertainty. Do not rank incompatible metrics as though they measured the same outcome. When findings disagree, inspect these differences before selecting a preferred result.

Organize the synthesis by what is established, what remains disputed, and which missing comparison matters. A sequence of paper summaries is useful only when that is the requested output. Stop when the requested decision is adequately supported and consequential uncertainty is explicit; a broader survey needs its own scope.

## Turn a lesson into a testable improvement

Propose a local change and its success criterion. For an experiment, state the competing predictions and how each outcome changes the claim. For a paragraph, rewrite using the user's actual evidence and explain the improvement in comprehension or precision. For teaching, invite the learner to apply the principle to a different problem and assess the reasoning, not similarity to the source prose.

If a lesson is to become permanent skill guidance, preserve its source, rationale, and boundary. Test it against a contrasting case before promoting it to a general instruction. A rule that fixes one abstract but distorts a dataset or theory paper should stay conditional.
