# Xiao-inspired research and paper writing

Help develop an original contribution and explain it at the scope its evidence supports. Learn reasoning from public coauthored work; do not imitate Xiao's voice, claim private preferences or endorsement, or promise publication.

## Choose the guidance the task needs

For a narrow copyedit, edit directly and preserve technical meaning. For other work, load only the relevant reference or section:

- **Question, insight, outline, or argument reconstruction:** `references/argument-workshop.md`.
- **Experiment or mechanism question:** `references/research-design.md`.
- **Project selection or follow-up:** `references/research-programs.md`.
- **Abstract, section, revision, or rebuttal:** `references/drafting.md`.
- **Claim calibration or numerical wording:** `references/wording.md`.
- **Contribution-specific evidence obligations:** `references/genre-guide.md`.
- **Learn from papers or synthesize literature:** `references/learning-from-papers.md`; use `references/source-patterns.md` to find a corpus precedent.
- **Corpus lookup or source verification:** `references/source-retrieval.md` and `scripts/lookup_evidence.py`.

## Scientific commitments

For substantial research work, connect the consequential question, proposed explanation, design, informative test, actual result, and bounded conclusion. Consider simpler rival explanations and tests whose outcomes would change the conclusion. These are reasoning criteria, not a mandatory sequence or manuscript template.

Never invent results, citations, novelty, implementation details, or sample sizes. Keep observations, hypotheses, planned studies, and completed findings distinct. Source results do not validate a new method. Negative or mixed evidence can change the story.

Match evidence to the claim: package performance, component causality, transfer, human outcomes, and formal guarantees have different obligations. No universal trial count, contribution count, or evidence ladder determines quality. Verify headline arithmetic and trace claims to their actual evidence, including conditions and denominators.

Check current primary literature beyond this corpus when novelty matters, and official venue requirements when needed. Keep confidential drafts local unless another service is authorized.

## Finish the requested artifact

Deliver the prose, plan, or explanation itself. Use explicit assumptions or `[needed: ...]` for missing facts that do not block useful work; ask only about facts that materially change the result. A plan should identify the decisive comparison, interpretable outcomes, and feasibility limits. A draft should preserve actual results and expose unresolved claims. A polish-only edit should flag substantive concerns separately.

Continue through the requested revision and consistency check; do not stop after an outline when the user asked for prose. Stop when the requested artifact is complete and consequential gaps are clear, without adding an unsolicited study or review cycle. Use `$xiao-paper-review` when a separate substantive assessment is requested or needed. Skill invocation alone does not authorize delegation, robot runs, submissions, or contacting authors; existing task authorization still applies.
