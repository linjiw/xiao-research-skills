# Research and writing rebuild — 2026-09-09

This revision makes the skills help develop a consequential research question and a coherent manuscript, while retaining evidence discipline. It also corrects methodological rules in the previous version.

The [public publication page](https://people.cs.gmu.edu/~xiao/#publications) was opened for context; this was not an exhaustive reconciliation against other publication databases or a claim that the snapshot includes every publication now available anywhere.

**This is a selected-section, argument-and-craft rereading across all 137 primary papers. It is not a new complete reading of every page, proof, equation, figure, appendix, or supplement.** The historical main-text records remain unchanged. Each paper now has a separate `craft_reread` field recording the date, consulted physical pages, source-version verification, bounded scope, and an analytical lesson. A page in that field means a selected passage was consulted, unless the scope explicitly says visual article reading. Extracting a PDF is not counted as reading it.

The shipped update contains analytical paraphrases and links, not source PDFs or extracted full texts. The lessons are this project's synthesis of coauthored work, not personal attribution to Xiao or evidence that a new method works.

## What changed and why

The new [argument workshop](../skills/xiao-paper-writing/references/argument-workshop.md) connects problem selection, competing explanations, design choices, discriminating tests, results, and conclusions. It includes a hypothetical robotics example that shows how a proposed study changes direction when a simple alternative explains the result. No synthetic outcome is presented as a measured result.

The drafting guide now teaches paragraph roles, known-to-new information flow, precise transitions, stable terminology, contribution framing, and consistent commitments across sections. Research design emphasizes the scientific decision an experiment resolves. Review assesses importance and coherence as well as numerical correctness.

The previous version sometimes converted corpus observations into universal rules. This update removes mandatory contribution counts, paragraph positions, acronym structures, one-variable-only follow-ups, fixed trial thresholds, and requirements that every supporting result occupy a table column. It removes aggregate writing-frequency claims whose underlying per-paper codings were not shipped. The historical study documents remain historical records, not current prescriptive guidance.

The evidence ladder is replaced by claim-specific warrants. Comparative performance, causal attribution, transfer, uncertainty, and formal guarantees are complementary dimensions. A well-controlled physical comparison can support a conditional ranking; its small size or single venue does not automatically reduce it to mere existence. Conversely, a large simulation cannot remove model bias through sample count alone.

The old VertiSelector critique was unsupported. The paper's page 6 describes angle spread as variance and displays 6.46 ± 26.21. The prior skills treated that spread as SD, inferred timestep pooling, and used an SD-based gap calculation. The new guidance asks for definition, units, and aggregation. Even a known SD greater than a nonnegative mean is possible. SD overlap is not a universal test of significance or equivalence.

## Validation and limits

All 14 package tests passed, and both skill entrypoints passed the skill-creator validator. A field-by-field comparison confirmed that all 137 historical records retained every original field. The package checks validate shared resources, provenance, page ranges, source keys, portable lookup, and distribution hygiene. The skill-creator validator checks skill structure. Neither demonstrates that the skills improve publication outcomes or reproduce any professor's judgment.

[Scenario walkthroughs](SCENARIO-WALKTHROUGHS.md) apply the revised guidance to a proposal, a coupled system comparison, an uncertainty example, a narrow copyedit, and a resource contribution. They are transparent self-checks by the editing assistant, not independent-agent or blinded behavioral tests. No independent agent was spawned for this revision.

The intended standard is a meaningful question, defensible reasoning, informative evidence, and clear original prose. The skills support that work; research quality still depends on the actual project and results.
